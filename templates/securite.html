🔒 Data Security, Privacy & Integrity

At ML2C CONSEIL, the security and confidentiality of your personal information are essential. We follow best practices to protect your account data. 

def validate_signup(email, password, pseudo):
    errors = {}

    # Validate pseudo (username)
    if len(pseudo) < 3:
        errors["pseudo"] = "Username must be at least 3 characters long."

    # Validate email format
    if "@" not in email or "." not in email:
        errors["email"] = "Invalid email address."

    # Validate password strength
    if len(password) < 8:
        errors["password"] = "Password must be at least 8 characters."

    return errors
